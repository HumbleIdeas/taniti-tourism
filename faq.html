Frequently Asked Questions

How do most visitors arrive?

Most visitors arrive by air, while some arrive by cruise ship at Yellow Leaf Bay.

What transportation is available on the island?

Visitors can use public buses in Taniti City, private buses elsewhere, taxis, rental cars, bikes, helmets, and walkable routes around Taniti City and Merriton Landing.

What activities are most popular?

Beaches, rainforest exploration, volcano visits, sightseeing in Taniti City, boat and bus tours, snorkeling, fishing tours, and Merriton Landing entertainment are highlighted for visitors.

What currency should I use?

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros, yen, and major credit cards.
