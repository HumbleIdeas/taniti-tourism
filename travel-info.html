Travel Information

Arriving

Most visitors arrive by air. Some arrive by cruise ship, which docks in Yellow Leaf Bay one night per week.

Getting around

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Taxis, rental cars, bikes, and helmets are available.

Visitor basics

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar, while many businesses also accept euros, yen, and major credit cards. English is common among younger residents, and the hospital has multilingual employees.

Quick answers

  • Power outlets: 120 volts
  • Drinking age: 18
  • Plan around national holidays
  • Use normal petty-crime precautions
