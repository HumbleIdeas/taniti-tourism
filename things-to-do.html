Things To Do

Taniti is known for beaches, rainforest, volcano sightseeing, fishing, snorkeling, and a growing entertainment district.

Beaches and bay

Spend time in Taniti City near Yellow Leaf Bay, where white sandy beaches surround the harbor.

Rainforest, volcano, and tours

Visitors can hike in the rainforest, take boat or bus tours, visit the active volcano, ride helicopters, or try zip-lining.

Merriton Landing

Merriton Landing is a developing area with pubs, a microbrewery, a dance club, movie theater, arcade, galleries, and other attractions.
