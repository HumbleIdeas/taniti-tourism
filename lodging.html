Lodging

Visitors can choose from an inexpensive hostel, a large four-star resort, family-owned hotels, bed and breakfasts, private homes, condos, and beach houses.

Suggested flow

Start by selecting travel dates, party size, budget, and location preference. Then compare lodging types and follow booking links provided by each property.

Choose by trip type

  • Families: hotels, resort, beach houses
  • Couples: bed and breakfasts, resort, private condos
  • Budget travelers: hostel and small hotels
  • Long stays: private homes and condos
